Materials

Polycarbonate.

Smooth, light, and beautiful. It breaks far less than glass, and it regulates temperature better. About 250 times the impact strength of glass.1

Clear, mirror, or tinted.

  • A wood stove, for a minus 30 winter.

  • Towable, or fixed in place.
