x-article-cover-1600x900.png  - X article cover (16:9). Primary, use this one.

Built by app/scripts/build-x-cover.mjs from plan portraits captured by
app/scripts/capture-plan-portraits.mjs against the live site. The three plates
are real plan diagrams with the captions the product printed - Horisont,
Trappelys and Klarhet, from the flat-roof glass set. Palette and type are
quoted from assets/brand-kit/palette.json.

Re-run both, in order:
  node app/scripts/capture-plan-portraits.mjs
  node app/scripts/build-x-cover.mjs
