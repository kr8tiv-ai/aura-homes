03 · Build

Build the home, then take the drawing

Compose your home out of rectangular volumes with real roofs, real openings and the floor-to-eave glass wall that makes a Nordic home a Nordic home. Move the sun and watch what the overhang does in June and what the low winter light does through the south glazing. When it is yours, press one button: the same object goes to the deterministic plan engine and comes back as a dimensioned floor plan with a room schedule — and an itemised account of everything the translation had to drop.

A massing and layout tool — not a structural design, not an energy model, and not a permit set. Everything runs in this browser: no account, no server, no key. Already answered the design questionnaire? That path asks how many bedrooms you want; this one asks what shape the house is.
