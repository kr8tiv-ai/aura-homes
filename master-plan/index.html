Master plan

A plan, told slowly.

The room is what you can ask for now. Everything after it is a direction, held lightly.

  1. 0101

    Now

    Second-life rooms.

    Beautiful second-life rooms, Hot Take tub units, and customs, made one at a time. Off-grid and solar builds are available.

    RoomsHot TakeCustoms

  2. 0202

    Grow

    Rooms that would grow.

    Leaf Skywalker grow rooms and aeroponic units would follow, as Aura Farms. The same room, with one long side turned toward a living wall.

    Aura Farms

  3. 0303

    Water

    Drawn from the air.

    Thirst Contact, water drawn from the air, would come later, after more research. It is not ready to place.

    Coming later.

    Water

  4. 0404

    Later

    A network, not yet.

    A host network for placing rooms is an idea for later. It is not operating, and it is not a promise.

    Location and care of the rooms would be part of that network if it is ever built. The network is not open.

    Seed pods, swaps, pickup, delivery, and repairs would be handled through the network.

    Coming later.

  5. 0505

    Stay

    Near the mountains.

    Micro-resorts, about half an hour from Banff and Canmore, would be built as showcases for the newest rooms.

    Coming later.

    You walk up to the deck.Pull the blankets onto the net.The water's ready.

  6. 0606

    Paradise

    Costa Rica, our second home.

    Building in harmony with nature comes easier there. The same care would go there when the work is ready.

  7. 0707

    Home

    Full eco homes, someday.

    Whole houses would come last, after the rooms have taught the rest. Still quiet, and still close to the ground.

One room at a time. Then a little more.
