Roadmap

Three arcs, honestly labelled.

The standing rule of this page: nothing unbuilt is written in the present tense. What runs is linked so you can use it; what is planned says planned; what is a later idea says so. The long-form version, with dates and evidence, lives in the open repo.

01

Now

The project workspace

What runs today, on the open MIT repo, with the tests that prove it.

  • A guided and pro editor over one durable project document — 3D, 2D plan, drawings, exports — with a 25-plan library whose every plan names its source and licence.

    Open the builder  →

  • A demonstration land-fit pilot that evaluates sourced example rules and explains why a sample parcel may not fit. It is not a live listing or permit decision.

    Try the land-fit pilot  →

  • An Alberta budget that reconciles to a published cost model to the dollar, with a DIY-or-hire toggle per line.

    See the budget  →

  • Contractor and manufacturer evidence workbenches for demonstration or user-supplied records, with source dates, visible expiries, and missing evidence shown.

    Check a team  →

  • Experimental transaction-mechanics contracts are deployed on X Layer testnet 1952 — escrow 0x4A77… and registry 0x1195… — with zero milestones and zero home records. The lab proves transaction execution, not lifecycle facts.

    Contract on OKLink  ↗

02

Next

The locality hub

Planned and being designed: a giant hub with bridges across — rolled out locality by locality, Alberta first.

  • Real listings with permissioned photos and full provenance — makers first, open land data beside them.

  • A regional cost basis beyond Alberta: public price indexes per region, every figure labelled with its source and date, never presented as a quote.

  • An evidence-backed, checkable contractor bench per trade, built from permitted public sources and partner records rather than scraped ratings.

  • Plot-of-land import: your parcel's setbacks, slope and services inside the editor, and a real site plan on sheet A1.

  • The internal concierge grows into a guide that reads your actual project and proposes next steps you confirm — it never acts alone.

03

Future

The HOMES ecosystem

A later idea, published in full and labelled planned: nothing below is live, and nothing here is an offer.

  • A planned property-holding and governance model for real eco homes. The legal vehicle, participant rights, and fee rules still need jurisdiction-specific design.

  • A user-owned network of eco stays — the long-term story: an Airbnb its guests and hosts own.

  • An RWA launchpad where owners fund their own eco-stay projects, with receipts public by construction.

  • Every claim carries its label on the ledger page — zeros rendered honestly until something is real.

    Read the HOMES ledger  →
