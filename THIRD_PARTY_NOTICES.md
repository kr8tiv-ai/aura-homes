# Third-party plan sources

Aura only includes an editable plan study when its source publishes clear reuse terms that permit commercial use. The original files are not bundled here; the builder links directly to their maintainers. Every instantiated study repeats its attribution, change notice, licence and source URL inside the saved `BuilderDocument` notes.

These studies are design intent, not construction, structural, energy or permit documents. Source drawings still require site-specific review by qualified local professionals.

## Open Timber Studio

- Source: [WikiHouse, maintained by Open Systems Lab](https://www.wikihouse.cc/design/what-is-wikihouse)
- Licence: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- Relationship: system-informed study
- Changes: Aura created an independent 6 × 3 m concept envelope. It does not reproduce a WikiHouse chassis, CNC file or construction detail, does not use the WikiHouse name as an Aura product name, and does not imply endorsement.

WikiHouse states that most of its files and information may be used, distributed and modified, including commercially, under Creative Commons ShareAlike; improvements to the system must be reshared and all designs require safe, regulation-compliant use and structural review.

## Liber’Tiny Study

- Source: [Liber’Tiny by Association Entropie](https://tinyhouse.asso-entropie.fr/manuel-auto-construction/)
- Authors credited by the source: Association Entropie, Christophe André and Nolwenn Le Nir
- Licence: [CC BY-SA 2.0 France](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/fr/)
- Relationship: dimensional adaptation
- Changes: Aura converted the published 6 × 2.55 m exterior dimensions into a simplified cold-climate `HomeSpec`. The loft, trailer, assemblies and plan details are not reproduced. The source describes its plan documents as beta.

## Open Farmhouse Study

- Source: [American Farmhouse by Jay Osborne, FreeFarmhouse.com](https://www.freefarmhouse.com/)
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution ShareAlike; the source drawing does not state a version. [Source drawing and licence notice](https://www.freefarmhouse.com/uploads/b/244cd9c0-4894-11ea-8aad-9b21e8eb84db/American%20Farmhouse%20-%20Jay%20Osborne%20-%20eBook.pdf)
- Relationship: dimensional adaptation
- Changes: Aura retained only the published 16 × 32 ft, two-storey envelope and replaced the assemblies, openings and deck with an independent Alberta concept model.

## Sources reviewed but not imported

Plans carrying non-commercial restrictions, “free” downloads without explicit reuse terms, unclear authorship, or terms that prohibit aggregation are not included as editable Aura templates. A public URL alone is not a licence.
