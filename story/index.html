Why we exist

Back to where we came from.

Aura exists to bring people back to where we came from. Back to our roots, more time outside, closer to nature.

An extension of your home, one you're proud of. Beautiful, and slick. A steel frame, smooth glass, and the land still doing most of the talking.

The aim reaches past the room. Less scarcity in an ordinary day. Better ways to live, to grow food, and to find water, with less reliance on the grid. The farm wall and the water come later. The master plan keeps them there, so this page does not pretend they are ready.

Why it started

Seen beside other people's hands.

The founder has been working on this for years. It started while travelling through Central America, seeing people build beautiful things in synergy with nature. Walls, shade, water, and a roof, each one listening to the place it stood in.

The shop is in Alberta. The lesson came from somewhere warmer, and it survived the trip.

Chapter one

A small shop, and the work.

Alberta. A small shop. The first units are being built now. A YouTube channel will document every step, the tidy ones and the ones with dust on them.

Nobody believes in you until it's too late, so we're just going to build it.

Nature as a gift

The part that was already here.

Morning coffee outside. Kids off their screens. A stretch of time surrounded by the beauty already around us.

Nothing here asks for a creed. Sit long enough, and the day gives itself back.

Founder

The mission holds the page.

The story is the mission, not the person. Years of looking, then a shop, then the decision to build it in public. A portrait can wait.
