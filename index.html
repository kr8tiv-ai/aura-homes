Off-grid, on-chain

Design it. Fund it. Build it.

Aura Homes is the agent that takes you from USDC on X Layer to the keys of an off-grid eco home — land, design, budget, escrow, and build, orchestrated end to end.

Scroll

01 · Land

Start with the land.

Real parcels, filtered for what a build actually needs: district dwelling minimums, aquifer reliability, distance to power, and septic soils. USDC in, title out.

02 · Design

The AI drafts your home.

A short questionnaire becomes a review-ready design package — SIP panels, checked against Part 9 and climate zone 7A, reviewed and stamped by licensed professionals before anything is built.

03 · Budget

A budget with nothing hidden.

Costed line by line from Alberta suppliers, with no middlemen. Ranges, not promises — LOW, MID, and HIGH, each with its basis shown.

04 · Escrow

Funds move when milestones do.

USDC held in escrow on X Layer, released on 2-of-3 approval. Alberta's statutory 10% holdback is modeled on-chain, and the build record is anchored as it happens.

05 · Build

Then you get the keys.

Permits, professionals, and materials, orchestrated to completion. The deck, the wood stove, and the hot tub under the spruce — the parts that make it a home, not a project.
