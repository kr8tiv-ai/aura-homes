From the sky

Aura H2O

Water from thin air. Literally.

There's water in the air around you right now, even on a dry day. An atmospheric water generator pulls it out. It cools the air until moisture condenses, filters it, and turns it into clean drinking water. No well, no water line, no truck, no awkward chat with the water delivery guy.

Get on the waitlist

Is this real?

Very real, and it works well. Militaries already run proper atmospheric water systems in the field, winter included. Plenty of people already use these at home and off grid. Today's commercial units work best when it's warm and a bit humid. Newer technology can pull water out of the air in Arctic conditions, and that's exactly where our research and development is headed. We're in Alberta. We need water in January too.

Where we start.

We're starting with the best commercially available units and building them into Aura rooms properly: insulated, powered, and good-looking enough that you'll give the tour before the coffee. It's an off-grid water source for nature rooms, acreages, glamping sites and anyone who'd rather their water didn't depend on a pipe from somewhere else.

Where we're headed.

We'll work with the companies building the best of this technology, keep upgrading as it improves, and run our own R&D so our units handle any weather Alberta throws at them. That includes the weather that makes you question your life choices.

The long game.

None of this is running yet, but it's where we're aiming:

  • Backyard bottling. A little bottling plant that pulls water out of the air and bottles it right where it's made.
  • A free water network. Clean water given away free, with sponsor labels on the bottles paying for it. People get paid to bottle the branded water. Yes, getting paid to make water out of air. We're as excited as you are.
  • Water where it's needed most. As the technology grows, we want it to bring water to people who don't have a reliable source.

Rooms first, then the water, and the rest grows from there.
