Materials

Polycarbonate.

Smooth, light, and beautiful. It breaks far less than glass, and it regulates temperature better. About 250 times the impact strength of glass.1

Clear, mirror, or tinted.

  • A wood stove, for a minus 30 winter.

  • Epoxy floors, poured smooth.

  • Railings and glass-style surrounds.

  • Gazebo tops, for stargazing.

  • Towable, or fixed in place.

1 Source: SABIC LEXAN Glazing Guide (https://ff.sabic.eu/uploads/resources/SABIC-SFS-6211-EN%20LEXAN%20Glazing%20Guide%20-%202021.pdf) and Covestro Makrolon data.
