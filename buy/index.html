The other path — buy, don’t build

Buy an eco home with crypto

You hold crypto. Somewhere out there a company will take it for a building. This page is the list of the ones that verifiably do — with what each of them actually accepts, how the payment really happens, whether a Canadian can use them at all, and the route from USDC on X Layer to their hands. Also the thirteen leads that looked right and were not, because a directory that only publishes its wins is an advertisement.

Read this before anything else

Aura facilitates. It does not sell homes, does not broker them, holds no custody of your funds at any point on this page, is not a party to any purchase you make, and carries no liability for it. It has no relationship — commercial, referral, or otherwise — with any company named here. They are listed because they are publicly documented as accepting crypto. That is the entire basis for their inclusion.

Nothing on this page executes a transfer, quotes a rate, or takes a fee. Every leg of every route is something you do yourself, in your own wallet, with your own eyes on the address. Verify each claim at the source link on the card before you move any money — and treat a wallet address that arrives over email or chat as unverified until you have confirmed it by voice.

The route

From USDC on X Layer to Crypto Emporium

Crypto Emporium names BTC among the assets it takes, and BTC is what the one live-verified X Layer hop actually settles into. Meson lists 44 other chains, but the Bitcoin leg is the one that was checked against its relayer API in this sweep, so it is the one drawn here. Three legs: get onto X Layer, convert, then send it yourself. Aura runs none of them.

Leg 01 — choose how you arrive on X Layer

Default is the OKX DEX bridge aggregator: non-custodial, no exchange account, and the rail X Layer’s own bridge page sends people to. If you are Canadian, read the KYC line before you pick the OKX exchange withdrawal — OKX does not serve Canadian residents.

Convert to BTC

Meson - USDC on X Layer <-> native BTC (one hop)

USDC / USDT0 / WETH / WBTC on X Layer → Native BTC on the Bitcoin mainnet (or 44 other chains)

  1. 1Connect wallet on X Layer
  2. 2Swap USDC (X Layer) -> BTC (Bitcoin), entering the provider's BTC address as recipient
  3. 3Meson relayer settles atomically

Custody

non-custodial

KYC

None

Fees

Meson advertises near-zero fee and near-zero slippage; a small relayer fee applies. Not independently rate-checked.

Time

Advertised as minutes-fast (typically ~1-2 min).

What was actually verified

LIVE-VERIFIED against Meson's own relayer API (relayer.meson.fi/api/v1/list and /limits): X Layer is present as id 'xlayer', chainId 0xc4 (=196), Meson contract 0x25aB3Efd52e6470681CE037cD546Dc60726948D3, with USDC/USD-T0/WETH/WBTC. Bitcoin is a separate live chain entry (chainId 'bitcoin:mainnet', native BTC). TWO CRITICAL CAVEATS I confirmed: (1) Meson's X Layer 'USDC' is 0x74b7F16337b8972027F6196A17a631aC6dE26d22, which is the LEGACY BRIDGED token, NOT Circle-native 0xB6CE...3061 - I verified on-chain that both contracts report symbol 'USDC', so they are visually indistinguishable in a wallet. (2) Hard per-swap limits: USDC min 1 / max 20,000, BTC min 0.0005 / max 0.1.

Where these two legs do not meet

Leg 1 and leg 2 do not necessarily mean the same token. Meson lists X Layer USDC as the legacy bridged contract 0x74b7…6d22; Circle’s CCTP mints the native contract 0xB6CE…3061. Both report the symbol “USDC” and are indistinguishable in a wallet. Nothing in this dataset verifies how to convert one into the other on X Layer, so check the contract address before you swap. That gap is real and it is not solved here.

Hard per-swap limits also apply: USDC min 1 / max 20,000, BTC min 0.0005 / max 0.1. A home does not fit in one swap.

You do this leg yourself, in your own wallet or account. This page does not run it, cannot run it, and never holds your funds. Technical note: No documented iframe/SDK widget found in Meson's docs. A static site can deep-link to meson.fi with prefilled params, but I could not verify an official embeddable widget.

Negative findings

What was checked and did not survive

A directory is only as trustworthy as the entries it threw away. These 13 leads were fetched and refuted — including the two that hurt most: Miradex, a genuine Romanian passive-house prefab maker whose only “crypto” matches were the word descriptor in Wix JavaScript, and Trademark Renovations in Calgary, whose 2018 headline is not a rail in 2026.

01

Miradex.ro (Romania — prefab timber-frame nZEB/Passive House kits): THE MOST IMPORTANT NEGATIVE. Cryptwerk lists it under construction companies accepting crypto, and it is a genuine eco-prefab maker (since 1992, 300+ projects, timber-frame house kits) — exactly the profile Aura wants. But its own site has zero crypto payment content. A regex on miradex.ro returned 16 apparent 'cripto' hits that are ALL inside the word 'des-cripto-r' in Wix JavaScript. Refuted.

02

High Performance Homes (Vancouver, WA): listed on the Cryptwerk construction directory; own site returned 0 visible crypto mentions. Also roofing/siding/windows, not homes. Refuted.

03

Energize Builders (Los Angeles): seed carried a B-press claim of '200+ crypto-funded projects'. Fetched energizebuilders.com — 0 visible crypto mentions on the live site. Refuted pending better evidence.

04

Trademark Renovations (Calgary, ALBERTA): the single most locally relevant lead in the whole sweep. CBC and the Globe and Mail reported in 2018 that it was the first Canadian general contractor to accept bitcoin and ether, and it does build custom homes. Its live site today mentions bitcoin, ethereum and cryptocurrency NOWHERE. Refuted — an eight-year-old headline, not a current rail.

05

cryptorealestate.com ('Crypto Real Estate'): Cryptwerk describes it as the largest global crypto real-estate marketplace with 1500+ listings. The live site is an unfinished GoDaddy template — 'Launching Soon' placeholders and stock filler copy ('Generate excitement', 'Say something interesting about your business here'). Refuted.
